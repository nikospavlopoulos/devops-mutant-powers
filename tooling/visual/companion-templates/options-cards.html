Where should TLS end?

Three ways to route shop.example.com. Click one, or several to compare; then tell me in the terminal.

A

At the gateway (recommended)

CDN → Envoy Gateway (TLS ends) → Service. One certificate to renew, one place for headers and limits.

B

Passthrough to the pod

The gateway forwards encrypted traffic; each app holds its certificate. End-to-end encryption, more certificates.

C

One gateway per namespace

Teams own their entry point; more load balancers and more to patch.
