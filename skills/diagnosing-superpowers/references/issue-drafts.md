# Issue drafts

Issues are drafted locally and filed by your human partner — never by you.

1. Fill `templates/issue.md` and write it to the case workspace as `issue.md`.
2. Show the exact text and the path. Do not open a browser, call a forge API or CLI, or post anywhere.
3. Say where it most likely belongs:
   - **This plugin's repository** — the default: a skill, hook, manifest or template of this plugin misbehaved.
   - **Upstream** — when the evidence points at a shared workflow skill whose text still matches
     obra/superpowers (see the plugin's `UPSTREAM.md`). Say so, so your partner can decide whether to
     report it upstream as well.
4. If a scrubbed bundle exists, give its path for your partner to attach.

Keep the redaction policy: nothing from the transcript leaves the workspace unless your partner approves
the exact text.
